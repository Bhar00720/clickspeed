Regular Clicking

Standard finger actuation delivering steady 6-9 CPS. Highly accurate for precision aiming in FPS titles.

Butterfly Clicking

Alternating two fingers on the same mouse button to register 15-25 CPS. Requires low debounce switches.

Jitter Clicking

Vibrating forearm muscles to trigger rapid 11-16 CPS bursts without dual-finger alternating techniques.
