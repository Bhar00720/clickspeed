school Typing Speed Intel

01

speed Reaction & Speed

Type Blaster forces you to type quickly under pressure. The descending data packets train both your reaction time and muscle memory, resulting in a higher overall WPM (Words Per Minute).

02

done_all Accuracy Matters

Hitting the right keys the first time is essential. Making a mistake means you lose time fixing it while packets continue to descend. Aim for high accuracy to maintain your combo multiplier.

03

sports_score WPM Benchmarks

For beginners, 30-40 WPM is average. Consistent practice can boost this to 50-60 WPM. Professional typists and gamers often hit 90-120+ WPM. Use this tool to benchmark your progress.

04

keyboard Touch Typing

To reach higher waves, learn to touch type—typing without looking at the keyboard. Feel the home row (ASDF JKL;) and train your fingers to reach the keys instinctively.
