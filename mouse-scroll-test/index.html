Mouse Scroll Wheel Hardware & Encoder Diagnostics

Optical vs. Mechanical Encoders

Mechanical encoders use internal physical brush contacts that suffer from wear and dust buildup over time. Optical encoders (such as TTC Gold Optical or Razer optical wheels) use infrared beams for zero mechanical contact wear and indefinite lifespan.

Detecting Reverse-Scroll Glitches

When scroll wheels jump up while scrolling down, it indicates encoder contact oxidation or dust contamination. Our real-time glitch monitor detects these single-tick reverse phase anomalies instantly to determine if your hardware requires cleaning or replacement.

Scroll Speed (NPS & PPS)

Notches Per Second (NPS) measures the raw rate of discrete mechanical steps actuated by your finger. High-end free-spin wheels (like Logitech MagSpeed or Razer Hyperscroll) can exceed 200+ NPS in unlocked inertia mode.
