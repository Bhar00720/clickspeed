1 What is an Aim Trainer?

An aim trainer is a specialized tool or game designed to help players improve their mouse accuracy, reaction time, and tracking skills for competitive FPS and RTS games.

2 How does Flick Shot training work?

Flick training spawns targets randomly across the screen. You must quickly snap your cursor to the target and click, building muscle memory for fast, accurate movements.

3 What is Tracking Aim?

Tracking aim involves keeping your crosshair locked onto a moving target. Our tracking mode simulates this by having a target move in unpredictable patterns.

4 How is the rank calculated?

Ranks are calculated based on your accuracy and performance in the specific mode. Ranks range from Turtle to Godlike, requiring >90% accuracy for the top tier.
