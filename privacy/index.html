Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 • Last Revised: October 21, 2026

1. Strict Zero Keystroke Logging Policy

Because Click Speed Lab provides diagnostic tools like the Keyboard Ghosting & NKRO Test and Spacebar Counter, user security is paramount. We maintain a strict zero-logging architecture:

Zero Remote Transmission Guarantee: All keydown, keyup, mouse click, and cursor trajectory events are consumed exclusively by client-side JavaScript memory in your local browser sandbox. No keystroke sequences, words, or character combinations are ever recorded, transmitted, or stored on our servers.

2. Local Storage Usage

Click Speed Lab uses your browser's native localStorage API solely to enhance your user experience:

  • Personal Benchmark Records: Storing your highest CPS score, peak polling frequency, or fastest reaction time.
  • UI Preferences: Saving your selected theme palette (Obsidian, Cyan, Emerald, or Purple).

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache.

3. Analytics & Performance Telemetry

We use privacy-conscious analytics tools (including Google Analytics 4) to monitor aggregated traffic patterns, popular test categories, and browser compatibility. These services collect anonymized IP addresses, browser types, screen resolutions, and referral sources. No personally identifiable information (PII) is captured.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Vendors

To keep all testing tools 100% free, we display non-intrusive advertisements from verified ad networks. Third-party vendors may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to our or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users possess rights to request data access, rectification, or erasure. As we do not collect personal accounts, email passwords, or database profiles, our data footprint is inherently minimal.

6. Privacy Inquiries

For questions regarding this policy or data practices, contact us at: help@clickspeedlab.com.
