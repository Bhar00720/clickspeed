About Click Speed Lab

Click Speed Lab is an open, zero-latency gaming peripheral diagnostic suite engineered to measure mouse sensor frequencies, mechanical switch debounce characteristics, keyboard matrix rollover, and human motor reflexes with scientific precision.

speed

Sub-Millisecond Timing Precision

Standard browser event timers like Date.now() suffer from OS millisecond quantization and clock drift. Click Speed Lab leverages the high-resolution performance.now() monotonic clock API, yielding timestamp measurements with microsecond-level (0.005ms) fidelity for accurate 1000Hz–8000Hz polling rate tracking.

shield

100% Client-Side Privacy

All keyboard keystroke matrix diagnostics, mouse trajectory telemetry, and CPS click recordings execute locally inside your browser process. No keystroke logging, form data snooping, or raw telemetry payloads are ever uploaded to remote servers. Your hardware testing is entirely private.

volume_up

Web Audio API Sound Synthesis

Instead of loading bulky WAV or MP3 audio files that trigger network lag, Click Speed Lab generates real-time mechanical switch click acoustics dynamically through the browser's native Web Audio API oscillators, bandpass filters, and noise envelopes for instantaneous sensory feedback.

Laboratory Peripheral Testing Standards

Competitive gaming peripherals operate at extreme tolerances. A delay of 4 milliseconds or a mouse wheel reverse-scroll glitch can cause missed sniper shots, botched weapon swaps, or failed Minecraft bridge blocks. Click Speed Lab was designed to replace ad-ridden, uncalibrated web tools with a clean, telemetry-driven workbench:

  • Mouse Polling Rate (Hz) & Jitter Analysis: Calculates real-time interval variance (Δt) between raw pointermove events. We verify whether your 1000Hz, 4000Hz, or 8000Hz esports sensor achieves its rated frequency and report standard deviation dropouts caused by USB controller bottlenecks.
  • Rotary Encoder Diagnostics: Analyzes raw delta values and directional phase sequences from optical and mechanical mouse wheel encoders (TTC Gold, Kailh, Alps) to flag erratic reverse-scroll hardware glitches caused by dust or switch oxidation.
  • Keyboard Ghosting & NKRO Verification: Tests the electrical matrix layout of your keyboard. Distinguishes between standard 6KRO USB HID boot protocol limitations and true diode-isolated N-Key Rollover without third-key electrical shorts.
  • CPS & Debounce Latency Benchmarking: Analyzes actuation velocity across 1s, 5s, 10s, and 60s windows, measuring butterfly double-clicking bursts against mechanical leaf switch debounce settings.

Click Speed Lab is developed and maintained by the engineering team at CoreLume Technologies. Our goal is to provide gamers, hardware reviewers, and enthusiasts with free, uncompromised hardware diagnostics.
