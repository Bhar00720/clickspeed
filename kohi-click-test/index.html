book Minecraft 1.8 PvP Combat Dynamics & Kohi Guide

01

timer Why 10s is the Kohi Standard

Unlike casual click speed tests that allow 1-second or 5-second sprints, the Kohi standard mandates a continuous 10-second duration. In real competitive PotPvP or Nodebuff matches, duels last minutes, and victory belongs to players who can sustain 10-14 CPS without hand cramping or aim degradation.

02

shield Knockback & Hit Immunity

Minecraft 1.8 applies a 10-tick (0.5s) damage immunity window. However, clicking at higher CPS (12-16) substantially reduces vertical and horizontal knockback because attack packet frequency resets your player velocity sprint state faster, initiating unstoppable W-Tap combos.

03

flutter Butterfly vs Jitter for Kohi

Butterfly clicking allows 14-22 CPS using two fingers on a single mouse switch with low debounce (0-4ms). Jitter clicking relies on forearm vibrations to reach 11-15 CPS with mechanical mice. Butterfly provides higher raw CPS, but jitter maintains slightly better crosshair centering for rod-swaps.

04

hardware Optimal Mouse Hardware

Tournament Kohi champions prefer mice with mechanical or adjustable optical switches that support customizable debounce time (e.g. Glorious Model O/D, Roccat Kone Pro, Razer DeathAdder V3, Logitech G Pro X Superlight) to minimize click latency.
