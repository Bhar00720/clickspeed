Frequently Asked Questions

1

What is the Mouse Precision Game?

The Mouse Precision Game is a steady-hand maze navigation challenge where you must move your cursor from the start to the finish line without touching the boundaries of the path.

2

How many levels are there?

There are 5 levels of increasing difficulty, starting from a wide path with gentle curves up to an ultra-narrow path with complex loops.

3

What happens if I touch the walls?

If you touch the walls, a collision is recorded, and your cursor progress must continue carefully, or you will rack up collision penalties affecting your final rank.

4

Does this game help improve gaming skills?

Yes, it trains micro-motor control and steady-hand accuracy, which are critical for precise aiming and tracking in many video games like first-person shooters and MOBAs.
