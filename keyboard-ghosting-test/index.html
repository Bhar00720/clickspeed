Mechanical Keyboard Ghosting, NKRO & Matrix Diagnostic Guide

What is Keyboard Ghosting?

Ghosting occurs when multiple keys are pressed simultaneously on a standard membrane or unoptimized keyboard matrix, and the controller registers an unpressed key (a "ghost"). Gaming keyboards use discrete diodes to guarantee anti-ghosting.

6KRO vs Full NKRO

6KRO registers up to 6 simultaneous alphanumeric keys over standard USB HID descriptors. Full NKRO (N-Key Rollover) enables unlimited simultaneous keystrokes with discrete matrix scanning.

Latency & Switch Diagnostics

This tool measures real-time switch hold duration, actuation consistency, and key debounce timing to detect failing or double-clicking mechanical switches.
